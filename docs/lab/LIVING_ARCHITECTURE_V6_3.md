## Implemented system

350 persistent modules and 18 unequal teams remain. Five analytical poses and membership are unchanged in identity, with new authored endpoints and local structural roles: shared decks, shear surfaces, recessed cores, capability panels and service fins. Eighteen rooms are deliberately not eighteen identical portals: five profiles differentiate filled sections, open atria, overhangs and partial roofs. The existing connected graph still supplies twenty inter-team edges; 54 explicit module-level relationships add local coordination. Strong dependencies have a substantial deck and two side interfaces; moderate and loose dependencies have progressively thinner single bridges. Bridge formation grows along the three-segment route, rather than revealing all segments at once. Endpoint sockets follow actual structural modules; local paths follow their moving panels.

The three connection layers share the existing instanced geometry: transient impulses over known local paths, restrained lasting dependency members, and thick multi-interface architectural bridges. This adds no extra draw call. Separate macro and micro clocks remain; signals disabled does not stop autonomous life. The same signal toggle disables both event illumination and event-driven structural response for paired causal proof.

| Event | Origin / affected | Known path | Structural consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initiative, 0–3 s of an 18 s clock, plus deterministic scroll windows | Team 1 → team 2; designated capability panel is the tracked source | Local module graph and dependency 1 | Staggered local extension and early agency convergence; local interfaces develop progressively |
| Collaboration, 6–9 s | Team 5 → team 14 | Cross-department edge 18 | Shared bridge widens and receiving panels extend |
| Decision, 12–15 s | Team 10 → teams 9 and 17 | Dependencies 9 and 19 | Selected panels retract/realign at the operating interface; distinct parts remain autonomous |

Time events are bounded/reversible interface activity. Durable relationship formation belongs to deterministic scroll progress; the demo does not invent a persistent business-state history. Idle pauses between events are intentional.

## Projection and lifecycle

The designated event panel's current world pose supplies the annotation anchor. An orthographic ray finds the visible surface on the actual instance. Both modules and bridge geometry participate in occlusion. Out-of-frame, hidden, inactive and editorial-window-covered targets suppress pointers. Overlay coordinates add the canvas offset, use CSS pixels independent of DPR, and are recalculated from current camera matrices. A diagnostic-only yaw control tests changing camera conditions; ordinary browsing retains a fixed camera and global transform.

Caption bodies stay in breakpoint-defined locations. Desktop lines route through reserved lower space; shorter desktop viewports have a stable upper-right caption position. Phone leaders begin above their fixed caption. No moving tooltip bodies, multiple competing annotations or arbitrary screen anchors. Real copy bounds and caption geometry are cached on scroll/layout/resize; idle updates project one anchor and write one SVG path/dot without layout reads. Reduced motion suppresses event pointers entirely. Context loss removes pointers; isolate toggles and post-final scrolling refresh overlay visibility even when the geometry pose does not change.
