# Information Architecture

The public site is organized around user intent rather than a biography-first portfolio.

## Primary navigation

1. `/` — positioning, proof, OAF preview, practice, perspective and contact entry point
2. `/oaf` — original decision model, principles, layers and applications
3. `/work` — practice: problems, diagnostic questions, typical outputs and cross-system leverage
4. `/writing` — perspective: editorial index and long-form notes
5. `/about` — role, operating philosophy and working principles
6. `/contact` — qualified contact path and direct message form

English mirrors the same structure under `/en/...`.

## Supporting routes

- `/privacy` and `/en/privacy` — contact-form privacy information
- `/writing/<slug>` and `/en/writing/<slug>` — individual essays / notes
- `/.well-known/security.txt` — security contact metadata

## Narrative

Home answers, in order:

**What do you do? → How do you think? → Where does it apply? → What is the evidence? → What can I read? → How do I start a conversation?**

The site avoids a service-catalog IA. OAF is the intellectual-property axis, Practice shows applied capability, Perspective builds thought leadership, About adds human/executive context, and Contact closes the conversion path.
